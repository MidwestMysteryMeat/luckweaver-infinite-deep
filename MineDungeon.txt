Gambling-as-Combat (Core)

Poker, Dice Runes, Blackjack, Roulette, Slot Gauntlets, etc.

Voxel Destruction & World Interaction

Free mining, tunneling, and structural collapse.

Skill Crafting System

Combine existing skills, spells, and alchemical components to forge entirely new abilities.

Classes (Cardsharp Rogue, Rune Dealer, High Roller Paladin, Chaos Croupier, Soul Banker, Lucky Bard, etc.)
